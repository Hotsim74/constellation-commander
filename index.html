How this works

  1. Start on the star board. Drag a star onto the one next to it to make a line of 4 or more. That earns you a drone.
  2. Drones land on the Board, the other screen, on your bottom row. Tap a lit square to put one down.
  3. When you both have 3 drones there, the Board opens. Take your turn there, move one drone, then come back and earn more.
  4. Hold 46 squares and you win.

  How it works, and where I guessed
  
Stars. Match 3 in a row or column clears tiles and nothing else. Diagonals only count from 4 up. Bigger shapes earn things. When you earn a drone, the free squares on your back row light up and you tap the one you want (or press Auto-place). Before the board opens you can also tap a back-row drone and tap another back-row square to shift it. With a full back row, at any point in the game, a stronger drone bumps the weakest one and the bumped one digs; the last claim drone is never bumped, so your guards always have a way to grow their land. If your back row is full, a new drone hovers over your star board instead and you tap the tile (or tiles) it removes.

Rocks to the other side. Match an L or T of two 3-lines and 3 rocks drop onto the other side's star board, on the top row. Both sides can do it.

  
Rocks are the blockers each side starts with, 13 by default, laid out as a shape picked for the game (the same shape on both boards), and nothing adds more. A board with no moves does not reshuffle while you have a drone to fly down. They pace the game: slow while they block the board, faster as they clear, a flood once they are gone. Dig them out by flying drones down, or blow them up.

  
The board opens when both sides have 3 drones on it. Then it is strict turns, one drone move per turn, on a chess clock, with a per-move limit (default 60 seconds) that passes the turn when it runs out. If you spend 30 seconds of your move on the jewels, the star board darkens until you act on the board. The clocks only run while both sides have a drone on the board; a side with none has its turn skipped. Clock at zero and you lose. Your star board never stops.

  
Walking. Select a claim, guard or warp drone and tap a square more than one step away: that becomes its destination. On each of your turns, if you do nothing for 4 seconds, it takes one step toward it and the turn passes. Act sooner and it waits. Steps avoid squares next to enemy guards; a guard stops when it runs out of your land.

  
Claim drone moves 1 square any direction. Whenever at least one square in the 3 × 3 around it is not yours, it can claim: unowned squares become yours, enemy squares become disputed, disputed become yours. Then it is used up. Fire it on a big empty patch for nine squares, or spend it on the last square you need.

  
Start. Each side owns the five squares in front of its wall and has two guards on patrol there. Starting guards do not count toward the 3 drones that open the board.

  
Guard drone moves 1 square, only onto land you own. Any enemy drone that stands on your land one square from a guard is removed at once; the guard stays. An enemy two squares away gets you an offer on your move: the guard jumps and takes it, and the guard is lost. A guard standing next to an enemy siege drone shields the wall: the siege drone cannot fire while the guard is there, and neither of them is harmed.

  
Strike drone moves up to 4 squares diagonally and may bend mid-move. It takes any enemy drone it lands on.

  
Hunter drone moves up to 4 squares in any direction and may bend mid-move, so it is the one strike piece that can leave its color. It takes any enemy drone it lands on.

  
Warp drone is one use. Warp to any square on the board: the 3 × 3 patch around it becomes yours outright, and enemy drones in it are removed. It cannot target a square with an enemy guard next to it, so a guard can never be warped away and the land beside your guards is warp-proof.

  
Siege drone moves one square a turn in any direction. Give it an order by tapping a square and it walks there on its own; when it reaches the enemy wall it fires on its own: one shot clears every enemy wall beside it, and with the Commander beside it the next shot takes the Commander and wins. It cannot take pieces and cannot be taken: strikes and hunters can't land on it, guards can't remove it, a warp leaves it standing. The only way to destroy it is to surround it with 4 drones on the squares around it. You can have two on the board at once; a third one you earn digs your star board instead of landing. It cannot fire while an enemy guard stands next to it: the guard shields the wall, and neither is harmed. Move along the wall to a square no guard covers.

  
Fly down. Once the board is open, on your turn any drone can drop to your star board instead of moving. Before it opens, drones wait on the back row; only a full back row sends the newest one down to dig. A claim drone removes 1 tile. A warp drone removes 1 tile, a guard up to 2, a strike drone up to 4, a hunter up to 6, a siege drone up to 8, all of your choosing. While you pick, a small board shows where everything lands, so you can set up a shape on purpose. The drone is spent.
