How this works

  1. You are trying to claim more of the sky than the bot. Your land is gold, the bot's is blue. 69 squares wins.
  2. Line up stars. Anything over 3 in a row gets you a drone, born where you made it.
  3. A drone is like a checkers or chess piece. You move it around to claim space and fight. Only scouts claim stars for you.
  4. Or win outright by walking a siege drone up next to the bot's Command drone, the C, with no guard beside it. Keep yours safe.
  5. Who takes whom. Raiders take what they land on, except a siege. Hunters fight with missiles only. Guards take scouts, hunters, warps and guards by moving next to them on your land. Every take earns a missile for your Command drone; scouts and hunters load from it by standing beside it. Tap any drone, yours or the bot's, to see its reach.
