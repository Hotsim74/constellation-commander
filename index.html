How this works

  1. One board, in the dark. You see your own land and what your scouts have found.
  2. Swap two stars to make a line of 4 or more. A drone appears where you made it, on your side.
  3. Move a drone one square, or ride its own star color for free. Scouts claim land and clear the fog.
  4. Hold 46 squares, or walk a siege drone to the enemy Commander, and you win.
Both are in Settings any time.
